Vision

Problem

The Silent Epidemic of Preventable Blindness.

Across Sub-Saharan Africa, the prevalence of vision impairment from glaucoma, cataracts, and diabetic retinopathy is alarmingly high and rising. Retinal cameras are scarce, specialists are concentrated in urban centres, and 90% of vision impairment cases worldwide are avoidable. The 2023 Ghana Eye Health Summit confirmed a growing local burden. The gap is not in treatment; it is in timely diagnosis.

Solution

A Software-First Mobile Eye Health Platform.

TrEye.ai is a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) — an AI-driven application that enables community health workers to conduct retinal screenings at scale using their existing technologies.

AI-Powered Diagnostics
Machine learning models detect five ocular diseases with accuracy comparable to clinical standards — including diseases endemic to Africa.
Guided Screening Workflows
Step-by-step in-app guidance enables community health workers to capture and submit good retinal images.
Decision Support
Instant risk stratification and referral recommendations.
Mass Screening at Scale
Automated software workflows allow a single health worker to screen entire communities, making population-level programmes economically viable.

Future Outlook

The Eye Is a Window Into the Whole Body.

Oculomics (the use of retinal imaging to detect systemic disease): is one of the most exciting frontiers in AI-driven medicine. Peer-reviewed research has shown that a single retinal scan can reveal markers for conditions far beyond the eye itself. TrEye.ai is positioned to expand into this space as the platform matures.

Cardiovascular Risk
Predicting heart attack and stroke risk from retinal vasculature patterns. Nature Machine Intelligence, 2022
Neurodegeneration
Early markers for Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease detectable in the retina before clinical symptoms appear. Lancet Digital Health, 2022 · Neurology, 2023
Metabolic & Renal Disease
Diabetes control, blood pressure, kidney disease, and hyperlipidemia signals — all accessible non-invasively from one image. Nature Biomedical Engineering, 2018 · Lancet Digital Health, 2020 · Eye, 2023
Hepatic & Cardiac Markers
Liver and gallbladder disease and coronary calcium scoring detectable from retinal photographs. Lancet Digital Health, 2021

For TrEye.ai, oculomics represents a long-term expansion pathway towards transforming a retinal screening tool into a multi-disease early-warning platform, accessible to populations who currently have no equivalent diagnostic option.
