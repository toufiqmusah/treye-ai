Why It Matters

Three pillars of
transformative impact

01
Radical Accessibility
By putting diagnostics in the hands of community health workers, OPTEOVISION reaches the last mile — rural communities, informal settlements, and underserved populations that specialist clinics never reach.
02
Early Detection Saves Sight
Most vision loss from glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy is irreversible once it progresses. Catching diseases early — before symptoms appear — is the only effective intervention. AI makes this scalable.
03
Cost-Effective Health System
Treatment at an advanced stage costs dramatically more than early intervention. By shifting the curve toward prevention, OPTEOVISION reduces the long-term burden on fragile health systems.
